# DRC Copper Belt

The Democratic Republic of Congo, with Zambia, forms the Central African Copperbelt — the world's most important source of cobalt and a top-tier copper region. Political instability, transport-corridor disruption, or resource-nationalism here tightens battery-metal and copper supply. This underpins the [[drc_copper_cobalt]] theme, priced through [[XCU_USD]] copper and the miners [[IVN.TO]], [[GLEN.L]], and [[FQVLF]]. See [[Copper]].
