# Uranium

No OANDA leg exists, so the system expresses uranium entirely through equities and ETFs: [[CCJ]], [[UEC]], [[URA]], and the Sahel-focused [[GLO.TO]]. Supply is concentrated in Kazakhstan, Canada, and Niger, which is why a coup or nationalization in the [[Sahel]] feeds the [[sahel_gold_uranium]] theme. Uranium also appears as a supply-security leg of [[russia_ukraine]] given Russian enrichment share. See [[Sahel]].
