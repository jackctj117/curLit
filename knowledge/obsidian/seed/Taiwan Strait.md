# Taiwan Strait

The ~100-mile channel separating Taiwan from mainland China, and the single most concentrated node in the global semiconductor supply chain — TSMC alone fabricates the majority of the world's leading-edge logic. A blockade or military action is simultaneously a supply-chain shock and a broad risk-off event: the yen bids ([[USD_JPY]] down), tech-heavy indices fall ([[NAS100_USD]], [[SPX500_USD]]), gold bids ([[XAU_USD]]), and the chip complex ([[TSM]], [[ASML]], [[SOXX]], [[SMH]]) reprices hard. This is the geography behind [[taiwan_semiconductor]]. See [[Semiconductors]].
