# Crude Oil

The waterborne benchmark [[BCO_USD]] Brent and the landlocked-US benchmark [[WTICO_USD]] WTI are the two crude legs the system can actually trade. Brent leads on any seaborne-supply threat because chokepoint and shipping risk hits it first; WTI carries lower beta to those events. Crude is the shared instrument that links [[energy_chokepoint]], [[oil_supply_shock]], and the crude spillover in [[russia_ukraine]] and [[red_sea_shipping]]. The petro-FX corroborators are [[USD_CAD]] and [[USD_NOK]]. See [[Strait of Hormuz]].
