# Platinum-Group Metals

Platinum ([[XPT_USD]]) and palladium ([[XPD_USD]]) — autocatalyst and industrial metals whose mine supply is overwhelmingly South African, making them acutely exposed to Eskom grid failures and deep-level mining disruption. This is the commodity core of [[south_africa_pgm_gold]], which also runs through the rand [[USD_ZAR]] and producers [[SBSW]], [[IMPUY]], and [[ANGPY]]. A grid collapse that idles smelters is a direct PGM supply shock.
