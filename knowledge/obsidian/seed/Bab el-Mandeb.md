# Bab el-Mandeb

The southern gate of the Red Sea, between Yemen and Djibouti, where northbound Suez-Europe traffic and southbound Asia traffic converge. Missile and drone threats to shipping here force diversions around the Cape of Good Hope, adding weeks of transit and repricing container freight. This is the geography behind [[red_sea_shipping]]; the crude and gas legs ([[BCO_USD]], [[NATGAS_USD]]) connect it to [[energy_chokepoint]]. Container and product carriers [[ZIM]], [[DAC]], and [[STNG]] are the most direct instruments. See [[Red Sea & Suez]].
