# LNG & Natural Gas

The tradable gas leg is [[NATGAS_USD]]. Global gas is regionally fragmented but increasingly linked by LNG cargoes, so it bids on two distinct shocks: Qatari LNG transiting the [[Strait of Hormuz]] (an [[energy_chokepoint]] event) and European supply loss when Russian pipeline or transit flows are cut ([[russia_ukraine]]). It also appears in [[red_sea_shipping]] and [[natural_disaster]] (Gulf-of-Mexico production and LNG-terminal outages). See [[Crude Oil]].
