# Red Sea & Suez

The Suez Canal and the Red Sea corridor carry roughly 12% of global trade and the shortest Asia-Europe sea route. When traffic reroutes around Africa, effective vessel supply tightens and charter rates spike — a container-shipping shock more than an oil shock. This is the core of the [[red_sea_shipping]] theme, expressed through carriers [[ZIM]], [[DAC]], [[CMRE]], [[SFL]], and [[MATX]]. The southern entrance is [[Bab el-Mandeb]]; the crude spillover links to [[energy_chokepoint]] via [[BCO_USD]].
