# Strait of Hormuz

The narrowest maritime chokepoint in global energy: roughly a fifth of the world's seaborne crude and a large share of Qatari LNG transit these ~21 nautical miles between Iran and Oman. Any credible closure threat reprices the waterborne benchmark first — [[BCO_USD]] Brent leads, [[WTICO_USD]] WTI follows with lower beta, and gas bids on the LNG exposure via [[NATGAS_USD]]. This is the physical geography behind the [[energy_chokepoint]] theme, and Iranian oil-supply stress ties it to [[oil_supply_shock]]. Tanker operators [[FRO]] and [[STNG]] carry direct freight-rate leverage. See also [[Crude Oil]] and [[LNG & Natural Gas]].
