# Strait of Malacca

The primary artery for Asian energy imports: crude and LNG bound for China, Japan, and South Korea funnel through this narrow passage between the Malay Peninsula and Sumatra. A disruption here is less about supply loss and more about re-routing cost and delay, but it still feeds the [[energy_chokepoint]] theme through [[BCO_USD]] and [[NATGAS_USD]]. Because so much of the tonnage is Asia-bound, a Malacca event overlaps the risk-off channel that also drives [[taiwan_semiconductor]] escalation dynamics. See [[Crude Oil]] and [[LNG & Natural Gas]].
