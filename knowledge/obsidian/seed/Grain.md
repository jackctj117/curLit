# Grain

Wheat ([[WHEAT_USD]]) and corn ([[CORN_USD]]) are the tradable soft-commodity legs, with ETF proxies [[WEAT]] and [[CORN]]. World grain trade is concentrated enough at the [[Black Sea]] that a corridor disruption there moves global prices, which is the mechanism behind [[black_sea_grain]]. Grain also spills into [[russia_ukraine]] (both are top exporters) and, through fertilizer, into the nitrogen and potash names [[MOS]], [[NTR]], and [[CF]].
