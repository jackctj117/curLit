# Copper

Industrial bellwether and electrification metal. The tradable leg is [[XCU_USD]]. Supply concentration in the [[DRC Copper Belt]] and in Chilean and Peruvian mines makes copper sensitive to African political risk and Andean disruption alike. It is the core instrument of [[drc_copper_cobalt]] and a corroborator elsewhere; the leveraged single-name exposure runs through miners such as [[IVN.TO]] and [[GLEN.L]].
