# Black Sea

The export gateway for Ukrainian and Russian grain — together a commanding share of world wheat and corn shipments — plus a significant Russian crude and product route. Blockade of Ukrainian ports or collapse of a grain-corridor agreement spikes soft commodities. This is the geography behind [[black_sea_grain]] ([[WHEAT_USD]], [[CORN_USD]], the ETFs [[WEAT]] and [[CORN]], and traders [[ADM]] and [[BG]]) and it overlaps the broader [[russia_ukraine]] theme through wheat and energy. See [[Grain]].
