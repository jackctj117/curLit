# Sahel

The semi-arid belt spanning Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger — a major gold-producing zone and, in Niger, a globally significant uranium source. A wave of coups and resource-nationalist moves has raised the risk of mine seizures and export disruption. This drives the [[sahel_gold_uranium]] theme: gold via [[XAU_USD]] and miners [[BTG]] and [[EDV.TO]]; uranium via [[CCJ]], [[UEC]], [[URA]], and [[GLO.TO]]. See [[Uranium]].
