Project scope:

I want to make an app that pulls in live ticker values of world currencies, cryptocurrencies, and rare metals.

I then want to pull down tickers from treasury bond ETFs and trusts like (FXY) which then AI will look at trends and recommend where to short and put based on news articles and world events in order to capitalize on the differences in the currencies and which will go, whether from puts or shorts.

I want to create a web UI that will be locally hosted.

See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` for the detailed technical design and implementation plan.
